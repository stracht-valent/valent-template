# valent

Valent Partners flair for Claude Code.

- **Logo banner**: the VP shield and the VALENT / PARTNERS wordmark, drawn in
  Valent teal and navy above the prompt.
- **Token burn**: beside the logo (or under it in a narrow terminal), this
  session's cost with what it would buy in burritos or McDoubles (the
  comparison turns over every few seconds), still bars for the plan's 5-hour and weekly limits with their reset
  times, and a lifetime total across sessions.
- **Valent accents**: a dark theme with Valent teal and navy for the prompt
  border, the Claude accent, permission prompts, plan mode and more. The mod
  switches to it once; pick another in `/theme` and that choice sticks.

## Commands

- `/valent` folds the banner to one line (`◤◥ VALENT PARTNERS $1.23 = 0.11 burritos 5h 42%`) and brings it back.
- `/valent theme` switches back to the Valent theme.
